Guía del profesor · Teacher guide (≈ 55 min)

Pacing guide: Presentational Writing · Presentational · Grammar: Formal register · Product: Currículum

  1. Modelo (7 min): Poll; read Camila's résumé. Press the highlight button: students notice the nouns and past-tense verbs that describe duties.
  2. Analiza el modelo (8 min): Pairs sort the items into résumé sections, then answer the MC individually.
  3. Marco (10 min): Walk through the sections table and the verb → noun fill-in. Point out the US vs. Spanish-speaking world differences in the ¡Ojo!
  4. Escribe (17 min): Students draft their own résumé with the frame (on paper or in a document). Early finishers add a «Voluntariado» section.
  5. Rúbrica (3 min): Read the rubric; students self-score their draft.
  6. Revisión entre compañeros (7 min): Swap; partner checks the list and marks every place where the style is not parallel.
  7. Boleto de salida (3 min): Exit ticket and confidence check.

Camila Reyes Soto, the employers and the email address in the model are fictional. Remind students not to put real home addresses or phone numbers on a projected or shared draft; a school email or «(número de teléfono)» placeholder is enough. Drafts from today and the cover letter from Day 153 are polished together on Day 157 (Project Workshop).

CAMILA REYES SOTO. Worcester, Massachusetts · school email. OBJECTIVE: To obtain a bilingual assistant position at a veterinary clinic during the summer. VOLUNTEER WORK: Neighborhood animal shelter (2023–present): cleaning cages and helping at adoption events. SKILLS: Patient, punctual and organized. Teamwork. Basic first aid. Camila usa dos estilos: sustantivos (cuidado, paseos, comunicación, limpieza, ayuda) o verbos en pretérito (atendí, manejé, organicé). Dentro de cada trabajo, usa un solo estilo.

Idea clave

Un currículum no es un párrafo: es una lista breve y clara. Usa un estilo formal y paralelo (el mismo tipo de palabras en cada lista):

  • Sustantivos: atención al cliente, manejo de la caja, organización de eventos
  • o verbos en pretérito: atendí a clientes, manejé la caja, organicé eventos

A résumé is a short, clear list, not a paragraph. Use a formal, parallel style: nouns (customer service, handling the register…) or preterite verbs (I served customers, I handled the register…).

¡Ojo!

En algunos países hispanohablantes, el currículum tradicional incluía una foto, la fecha de nacimiento y el estado civil. En Estados Unidos no se incluyen. Adapta tu currículum al país donde vas a trabajar.

In some Spanish-speaking countries a traditional résumé included a photo, date of birth and marital status. In the U.S. these are NOT included. Adapt your résumé to the country where you'll work.
